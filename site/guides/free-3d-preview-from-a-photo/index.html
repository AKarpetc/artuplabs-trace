Free 3D preview from a product photo

Upload one photo of a chair, sofa, table or lamp and get a 3D model you can rotate, place in your room in AR on a phone, and download as a GLB for your Shopify store.

Published 2026-10-08 · Last checked 2026-10-08

Try it now

One free preview per person per day, while the free previews last. No sign-up, no card.

Make a free 3D preview

How it works

  1. Upload one product photo. JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB: one item, plain background, the whole item in the frame.
  2. Wait about 3–4 minutes. The page shows the progress and a timer. You can copy the link and come back later; it works for 7 days.
  3. Rotate the model and view it in AR. Drag to rotate. On a phone, View in your room opens AR on Android and iPhone.
  4. Download the GLB. Upload it to the product's Media in your Shopify admin; Shopify shows it in 3D and AR.

What you get

A textured 3D model like this one, made from a single photo with no manual edits.

The fine print

  • Time: generation took 135–209 seconds per model in our tests, plus a short optimization step and any queue.
  • Size in AR: approximate — a photo doesn't carry real dimensions. In the app you set each product's real height.
  • Limits: glass, mirrors, very thin legs, detailed fabric and glossy finishes come out worse. What works and what fails.
  • Your photo: used only to make the preview; the photo and the model are deleted after 7 days. An email is optional and used only to send you the link.

From one preview to the whole catalog

The free preview is one model. The app does the same for every product, from the photos already in your store, with a review step before anything goes live — and lets shoppers place several products together in their room.
