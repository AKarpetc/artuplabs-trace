AR for furniture stores on Shopify: what it costs

Shopify shows 3D models and AR on its own. The real cost is getting a model for every product. Here are the options — Shopify's built-in media, a 3D artist, scan apps, Blender and photo → 3D apps — and what each one costs, where there is a source.

Published 2026-10-08 · Last checked 2026-10-08

The cost has two parts

  1. Showing the model and AR. Shopify does this itself: upload a GLB to a product's media and Online Store 2.0 themes show it in 3D, with AR on iPhone and Android. (source: Shopify Help: product media types)
  2. Getting the model. This is where the cost is. Most furniture stores don't have 3D models of their products.

What we know about photo → 3D costs

In our tests one generation took 135–209 seconds, and the compute cost of a model was about $0.53 at our provider's price list. Measured That is what the generation costs to run, not a price you pay — app pricing adds hosting, storage, retries and support.

What it returns

We haven't measured the effect of AR on conversion or returns in furniture stores, and we won't quote a number we can't source. The practical test is cheap: add 3D to a handful of best-selling products, keep the rest as they are, and compare their add-to-cart rate and return reasons in Shopify Analytics over a few weeks.

Questions

Does Shopify charge extra for 3D models in product media?

3D models are a standard product media type in Shopify, like images and video; Shopify's help pages on 3D media don't list a separate fee. The cost is getting the model.

How much does a 3D artist charge per furniture model?

It varies with the item's complexity, the detail you need and the artist; we haven't found a source we trust for a typical figure. Ask several artists for a quote on the same product.

Do I need an app to show AR?

Not for one product at a time: Shopify themes show a 3D model and open AR on iPhone and Android. Apps add things like making the model from a photo or placing several products together.
