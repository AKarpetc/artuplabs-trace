Photo tips that make a better model

  • One object. Only the item you want in 3D — no second chair, no plant, no person in the frame.
  • Plain background. A white or evenly coloured wall or a studio cut-out works best; a busy room scene confuses the shape.
  • The whole item is visible. Nothing cropped at the edges, legs and arms not hidden behind other things.
  • A three-quarter view. A slight angle that shows the front and one side gives the generator more shape to work with than a straight front shot.
  • Even light. Avoid hard shadows and strong reflections.

Honest limits

One photo shows one side of the item, so the generator has to guess the back. That works well for most chairs, sofas, tables, lamps and cabinets. It works worse for:

  • Glass and mirrors — transparent and mirrored surfaces come out opaque or smeared.
  • Very thin legs and wire frames — thin parts can come out thicker, bent or merged.
  • Detailed fabric — fine weave, bouclé loops and pleats come out as a flat texture, not real geometry.
  • Glossy and reflective finishes — reflections of the room get baked into the colour.

What you can do with the model

  1. Add it to a Shopify product. In your Shopify admin, open the product and drop the GLB file into Media. Shopify shows 3D models on product pages in Online Store 2.0 themes. Step by step: How to add 3D models and AR to Shopify.
  2. Check it in AR. Open this page's link on your phone and tap “View in your room”.
  3. Do it for the whole catalog. The app does the same from product photos in bulk, inside your admin, with a preview to accept or regenerate before anything reaches your store.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. One free preview per person per day, while the free previews last. No sign-up and no card.

What happens to my photo?

It is used only to make this preview. The photo and the model are deleted after 7 days. If you leave an email, we use it only to send you the link.

Can I use the GLB file in my store?

Yes — the model is made from your photo for you. Check that it looks right before you publish it, and use photos you have the right to use.

Why does it take minutes, not seconds?

The generator builds a full textured 3D model. In our tests one model took 135–209 seconds to generate, plus a short step that makes the file small enough for phones.
