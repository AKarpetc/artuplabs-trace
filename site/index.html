What ArtUp Trace does

Built for teams who have to show that every requirement was verified, without leaving Jira.

  • Coverage

    See which requirements are covered by verification issues (tests or tasks) through native Jira issue links. You choose which issue types and link types count.

  • Suspect links

    When a requirement's summary or description changes after its link was confirmed, the link is marked suspect. Review and confirm one link or a selection at once.

  • Baselines

    Freeze a snapshot of your requirements and their links, then compare any two baselines to see what was added, removed or changed.

  • Export and issue panel

    Download a CSV export as a file for audits and reviews. The issue panel shows traceability right on the requirement.

Your data stays on Atlassian

ArtUp Trace is an Atlassian Forge app that runs on Atlassian. There are no ArtUp Labs servers in the path.

  • Runs on AtlassianNo external servers, no data egress.
  • Read-only in JiraReads Jira as the current user and never writes to your issues.
  • Least privilegeScopes: read:jira-work, read:jira-user, storage:app.
  • No trackersNo third-party analytics in the app or on this site.
  • 26 languagesFollows your Jira language, with light and dark themes.
