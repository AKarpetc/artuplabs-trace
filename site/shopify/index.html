AR Room · ArtUp Labs app for Shopify

Product photos to 3D. Products in the shopper's room.

Turn product photos into 3D models and let shoppers place several products in their room in AR. Review each model in your Shopify admin, set the item's real height and add it to the product's media in one click. Shoppers collect products into their room, view up to five of them together and add the whole set to the cart.

From a product photo to the shopper's room

You make the models in your Shopify admin; a theme block puts the room on your product pages.

Generate

A 3D model from the photo the product already has

The app's Products page lists your products with their 3D status. Open a product that has a photo and click Generate 3D.

  • The product's main photo is used; a single item on a plain background works best.
  • Generation usually takes about 3 minutes, and the page updates on its own.
  • A product without a photo shows No photo until you add one in Shopify.
Review

Preview every model, then accept, regenerate or reject it

When the product shows Preview ready, rotate the 3D preview to check it. Enter the item's real height in centimeters (1 to 500 cm): the model is scaled to it, so the product appears at true size in AR.

Accept adds the model to the product's media in Shopify. Regenerate makes a new model for the same product (it counts as a new generation); Reject discards the preview.

No code

Two buttons on your product pages

In the theme editor click Add block and choose Apps → AR Room button — or use the button at the bottom of the app's Products page, which opens the theme editor with the block already added, so you only click Save.

  • View in my room and Add to my room appear on products that have a 3D model and stay hidden on the others.
  • Change the button labels and color in the block settings.
For shoppers

Several products together, then the whole set to the cart

Shoppers click Add to my room on product pages. The room holds up to 5 products and is kept in the shopper's browser. View in my room opens the room page with the current product and the ones added before, in 3D; shoppers move and rotate them.

Add all to cart opens your store's cart with the products from the room.

Where shoppers see AR

The room page opens in the browser at artuplabs.com/room/ — nothing to install on Android or desktop.

  • AndroidChrome on a phone or tablet with ARCore: the room page offers AR, so the products can be placed in the real room.
  • iPhone and iPadSupport through the ArtUp Labs App Clip is rolling out (pending Apple approval); until then the room is shown in 3D without AR.
  • DesktopThe room in 3D, with a QR code to open it on a phone.

Set up in five steps

The app opens inside your Shopify admin; no separate login or account is needed. The guide walks through every step →

  1. Install the app.

    Approve the access request in Shopify.

  2. Choose a plan.

    Click Choose a plan, pick one on Shopify's plan page and approve it. Every plan starts with a free trial.

  3. Generate a 3D model.

    Open a product with a photo and click Generate 3D.

  4. Check the preview and accept it.

    Enter the real height and click Accept; the model appears in the product's media.

  5. Add the button to your theme.

    Add the AR Room button block to the product template and save.

Your store's data

The app keeps only what it needs to make models for your store. Privacy Policy →

  • No shopper dataThe app does not collect personal data of your customers.
  • Only the photo URLTo build a model, the URL of the product's photo is sent to fal.ai.
  • Short storageGenerated model files are deleted from our storage within 14 days; accepted models live in your store's media.
  • Deleted on uninstallAccess tokens are deleted right away; generation records and model files when Shopify sends the data deletion request.
  • Narrow accessScopes: read_products, write_products, write_files.
